Questions & Answers

Does The Meridian Academy teach regular school subjects and laboratory tracks? +

Yes. Our primary foundation is absolute textbook board mastery across Mathematics, Science, and English for Classes 6th to 12th (CBSE, ICSE, HPBOSE). Alongside academic classes, students participate in hands-on STEM & IoT sessions to connect theory with real practical applications.

Who personally leads and directs the academic classes? +

All curriculum pathways and technical programs are directed and supervised by Dr. Deepak Saini, who holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from PEC Chandigarh, an M.Tech & B.Tech in ECE, NPTEL Deep Learning Certification, and brings 7+ years of university lecturing experience at Thapar and Chitkara Universities.

Yes. We offer specialized thesis guidance for final-year BCA, MCA, B.Tech, and M.Tech students in Machine Learning, IoT systems, Python algorithmic models, and IEEE/Scopus format documentation.
